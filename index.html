Variable Slit HDR Technology

Our flagship technology utilizes an active multi-state aperture sweep to achieve unprecedented dynamic range in spectral data acquisition. By dynamically controlling the entrance slit width, we optimize exposure levels across complex spectral lines.

  • Hybrid optical skeleton utilizing pultruded carbon fiber
